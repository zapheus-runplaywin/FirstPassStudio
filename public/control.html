Each division's finals format is set on the competition setup page, and the teams come from the ladder. When PlayHQ has the finals games, their scores move the winners on by themselves. PlayHQ often has no finals fixtures, so pick each winner here (the score is optional). A result from PlayHQ always wins over one picked here.

The scoreboard is http://localhost:8080/scoreboard.html: add it on the Output page.

Switching between Follow PlayHQ and Manual (here, on the Stream Deck, or by pressing a score button) also turns Follow the PlayHQ scorer's clock on or off to match. Change the clock afterwards if you want it different.
