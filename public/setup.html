Competition setup

Everything about a competition that doesn't come from PlayHQ: its PlayHQ link, names, game rules, colours, font and logos. Each competition keeps its own setup. The one marked In use is what every graphic, the control page and the Stream Deck use. Back to the page links

Competitions

A new competition starts as a copy of the settings and colours of the one you're editing (not its logos).

PlayHQ

Open the organisation that runs the competition on playhq.com and copy the address from the browser. For a carnival like the AMMNA nationals, or GIG WANL (the WA Netball League), that's the organisation the competition is listed under.

Names on the graphics

Game rules

Halves give the control page H1 and H2 buttons and the shooting stats say "Half time" and "Full time". Without super shots the Super columns are left off the shooting stats and top shooters (the control page keeps its +2 and −2 buttons). Set the finals marker to 0 for none.

Game day

These are the starting points; the control page can still change the court, pick a game by hand or force a round. On a carnival day with several rounds, "this round" moves on to each round as its first game comes up.

Colours and font

Click a swatch for the colour picker, or type or paste a colour into its box as HEX (#7f4092) or RGB (127, 64, 146 or rgb(127, 64, 146)).

Brand kit colours

Paste the colours from a brand kit, PDF or website: any text with HEX or RGB values in it, such as "Purple #7F4092, RGB 127 64 146". They're saved with this competition and show in every colour picker, ready to click.

Competition logo

Shown above the title banner and in the corner of the ladder. A PNG with a transparent background looks best.

Team logos

Logos are matched to PlayHQ team names by their file name ("Perth Coastals.png" for Perth Coastal), so name each file after its team. Any team without a logo shows its initials in a circle.

Teams on PlayHQ

Pick another logo for a team whose file name doesn't match. Team colour is optional: a team with one gets it behind its name on the scoreboard and behind its logo and name on The Clash; a team without one uses the competition's colours. Use the colour picker, or paste the team's colour as HEX or RGB. When teams have similar names ("Stormz" and "Stormz 2"), you're offered the same colour for all of them.
