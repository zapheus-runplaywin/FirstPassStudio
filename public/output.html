Output

Studio's scene: add sources, order the layers, and show or hide them here or from the Stream Deck. The Mac app sends the scene to extra displays, a Blackmagic UltraStudio or DeckLink, or NDI® (below) for your switcher.

Scene View

Live View is off

The Scene View shows the scene as it is right now, whether or not your switcher has it on air. It runs every page in the scene a second time, so turn Live View off on stream nights if the Mac is busy. The dashed box is the selected source. Pop out shows the Scene View in its own window that you can resize, move to another screen or make full screen, with On/Off buttons for the sources floating in it; the one here turns off while it's open, and closing it brings it back. Scene View sound plays the scene's sound here, with or without an output on. A display, Blackmagic or NDI output goes silent while it does, so the sound never plays twice. It needs Live View on, and moves back to the outputs when this page closes. Only one Output page plays it at a time: click a Scene View to bring the sound to it. The line above says why it's silent, if it is.

Scenes

Video output

Sending the scene to a display, a Blackmagic device or NDI needs the FirstPass Studio Mac app (this copy of Studio was started another way).

Switcher key settings: leave Straight off and turn the switcher's Pre Multiplied Key (shaped) on; clip and gain are then not used, and soft edges and shadows key cleanly. With Pre Multiplied Key off, clip and gain cut into the soft edges and the fill's edges go dark. Tick Straight only if the switcher can't do a premultiplied key.

Turn the output off to change these settings.

The scene fills each display full screen, scaled to fit. Fill is the scene over black; key is white wherever the scene shows and black where it's see-through (grey where it's partly see-through). Sound plays from the fill or chroma display's window through the Mac's audio output. Each output opens full screen in its own window: leave full screen (the green button or ⌃⌘F) to move it to another display or get at other apps. To turn it off from the display itself, click it and press Esc, or close its window. Blackmagic: needs Blackmagic Desktop Video installed, and nothing else (such as Media Express) using the device; the scene plays at the video format's frame rate, its sound through the Mac's audio output. Pick the format the switcher runs at. NDI: needs NDI's runtime for Mac (free, ndi.link/NDIRedistV6Apple) installed on this Mac. The sources show up on the network as this Mac's name (Source name Fill) and (Source name Key), or (Source name Chroma), or just (Source name) with transparency; pick them on the switcher or in its NDI converter. Each sends at the video format's frame rate only while something is receiving it. The sound plays through the Mac's audio output, not over NDI. Stream Deck: /deck/output/toggle (also on and off) shows or blanks the output without changing this setup.
