Sponsors

The sponsors of the competition in use, by tier. Each sponsor has its logos, website, offer and deal dates, and a QR code to its website. A sponsor can be shown on more than one competition, at its own tier in each, and is kept once: change its logo or link here and it changes everywhere. Back to the Graphics page

These settings were saved by a newer version of FirstPass Studio, so they can't be changed here. Update Studio to change them.

Tiers

TierShare of screen timeWhen this tier has no sponsor

Pull up and Pull down fill the empty tier's spots with a sponsor pulled up from the tier below or down from the tier above, and keep going past empty tiers. Skip tier leaves the spots out. Advertise vacancy puts the vacancy card below in the tier's spots where a card fits (break cards and the sponsor wall); on the scoreboard tab and "brought to you by" tags it skips. The share sets how screen time is split between tiers when a sponsor has no target of its own.

Vacancy card

Shown in an empty tier's spots when it's set to Advertise vacancy, to find this competition a sponsor. Without a link or email it's never shown.
