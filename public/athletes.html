Athletes

Photos of each team's athletes, for the position match ups graphic. Pick a team to see every athlete PlayHQ has in its line ups this season, then add a photo to each one. A photo is saved under the athlete's name in the competition's photos folder (competitions/…/photos), so it matches that athlete in every game, team and division: an athlete who plays in more than one division needs only one photo. Without a photo, graphics show a generic silhouette for the athlete's gender (from PlayHQ, or picked under the athlete). Photos are made smaller before they're saved; cut-out PNGs with a transparent background look best. A photo goes on the stream and the Socials pictures only once Approved for photo use is ticked: tick it when the athlete (or their parent or guardian) has agreed to their image being used.
