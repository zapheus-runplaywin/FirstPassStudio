Socials

Pictures of a game night for Instagram and other social media, before it (Upcoming) or after it (Results), to Save as PNGs or Share to your phone. Click a preview to see it bigger. More in the guide

Saving and sharing the pictures needs the FirstPass Studio app on the Mac. The previews here are what they will look like.

Sizes:
Tick the kinds of picture this set shows, on Upcoming and Results.
Options

Upcoming is for before the night: the fixture, a match up for each game, who is on the stream and a polaroid. Results is for after: final scores, a full time card for each game, the ladders and the top shooters.

Expand for info

A night with results opens on Results. Template is the look of every picture: Default is the stream's own, and the others each bring their own shapes and lettering. Star (☆) the ones you use and tick Favourites only to list just those. Show lists every picture, or only the kinds of picture in a picture set you make (New picture set, ✎ to change one). Division lists one division's pictures, with the ones for the whole night. Colours change every picture in any template: sets made from the competition's main, highlight and accent colours (Competition colours is the stream's own look), or your own with New colour set. Under Options, the FirstPass Studio mark sits bottom right and the PIVT logo bottom left; untick either to leave it off. Whether each picture carries the sponsor strip (your naming, elite and major sponsors) is remembered for next week. Who is on the stream comes from the Production Planner. As a carousel saves the ticked pictures as one Instagram post in the order shown above Save (drag one, or ‹ ›, to move it): one size, up to 20, each marked 1/6, 2/6… top right with an arrow to swipe on, in a folder of their own.
