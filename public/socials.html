Socials

Pictures of a game night for Instagram and other social media, before it (Upcoming) or after it (Results), made from the fixture, results and ladders Studio already has. Pick the night and the sizes, untick what you don't want, then Save them as PNGs or Share them to your phone with AirDrop, Messages or Mail.

Saving and sharing the pictures needs the FirstPass Studio app on the Mac. The previews here are what they will look like.

Sizes:

Upcoming: the night's fixture, a match up for each game, who is on the stream (from the Production Planner) and a polaroid for a match photo. Results: the night's final scores, a full time card and a polaroid with the score for each game, each division's ladder and the night's top shooters. A night with results opens on Results. Template is the look of every picture: Default is the stream's own, and the others each bring their own shapes and lettering. Colours change every picture in any template: sets made from the competition's main, highlight and accent colours (Competition colours is the stream's own look), or your own with New colour set. The FirstPass Studio mark sits bottom right and the PIVT logo bottom left; untick either to leave it off. Whether each one carries the sponsor strip (your naming, elite and major sponsors) is remembered for next week.
