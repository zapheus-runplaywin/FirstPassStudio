Recordings

For each PlayHQ game on the scoreboard while on air, Studio records what PlayHQ said and what went to air (the graphics, not video or sound), to play the game back; and for the night's other games it follows, what PlayHQ said, to put graphics over a video of them later. More in the guide

Nudge

To put the graphics over a video of the game: jump to the quarter the video is on and Pause, cue the video on that quarter's first centre pass, press Play on both together, then nudge until the score changes with the goals.

PlayHQ's answers from the game are replayed, moved to now, so Studio runs as on a real game night: the scoreboard, sequences and outputs all work. Stop puts the scoreboard back as it was.

4K replay

Nudge

The game's graphics go to every output as they went to air, with the Output page's 4K on, to key over the camera recording.

Expand for info

Studio doesn't play the video. To sync by hand: pause the video on a moment you can see (the first centre pass), Jump there to the same moment, then press Play on both together and nudge until they match. While a replay runs the live scene and scoreboard are kept as they were and can't be changed.

With the video on a HyperDeck, Follow its timecode instead: the HyperDeck's play, stop and scrubbing drive the graphics, so they never drift. Type its IP address (on its setup page, or in HyperDeck Setup). To sync once, stop the HyperDeck on a moment you can see, pick the same moment above and press Jump there; the nudge moves the graphics against the video. The sync is kept for each recording. Or send the player's SDI into the UltraStudio's SDI input (the HyperDeck's monitor output is enough) and follow the timecode it carries. HDMI carries no timecode.

Saved demos

Demo game

Plays as one of your competition's games: their teams, athletes, logos and colours, with the recorded score, clock and moments. Pick the game whose teams and athletes to show.

Athletes are matched by position, so the recorded GS's goals are your GS's. A demo needs the internet.

Expand for info

Anyone with no match is left unnamed and their goals go to the team. Swap puts the teams the other way round, so either can be the winner. Your competition's teams and athletes come from PlayHQ, which is why a demo needs the internet.

Recording

(0 to 60).

Expand for info

A recording starts when its game is due on the scoreboard, 10 minutes before its scheduled start (Competitions setup's automatic game selection), and stops this long after full time, or when the scoreboard moves on to another game.

Games

  • Replay: the game's graphics exactly as they went to air, to key over a 4K recording of the game (turn on 4K on the Output page).
  • Practise: the game again as if it were live now, with today's scenes and sequences, to redo the production, rehearse or try a sequence. Your presses on the night aren't replayed.
  • Demo: a practice game dressed as one of the competition in use's games, to show Studio to another league. Needs the internet.

(0 to 60).

Expand for info

Practise and Jump to Start put the game this long before its first centre pass, time for a pre-game sequence. A pre-game sequence due before then is skipped, as on a night Studio was opened late.

Run game-timed sequences again (Practise, Demo and saved demos): each start and jump forgets which of the game's moments have already fired, so its pre-game, break and full time sequences go to the head of Output 1's run order again, even for a game you've practised before or played live tonight. Untick it to leave moments that have already fired alone. A 4K replay doesn't run sequences: it plays what went to air.
