4K replay

Nudge

The night's graphics go to every output exactly as they went to air, with the Output page's 4K on for 2160p, to key over the 4K camera recording. Studio doesn't play the video. To sync: pause the video on a moment you can see (the first centre pass), jump here to the same moment, then press Play on both together and nudge until they match. While it runs the live scene and scoreboard are kept as they were and can't be changed.

With the video on a HyperDeck, Follow its timecode instead: the HyperDeck's play, stop and scrubbing drive the graphics, so they never drift. Type its IP address (on its setup page, or in HyperDeck Setup). To sync once, stop the HyperDeck on a moment you can see, pick the same moment above and press Jump there; the nudge moves the graphics against the video. The sync is kept for each recording. Or send the player's SDI into the UltraStudio's SDI input (the HyperDeck's monitor output is enough) and follow the timecode it carries. HDMI carries no timecode.

Demo game

Plays as one of your competition's games: their teams, athletes, logos and colours, with the recorded score, clock and moments. Pick the game whose teams and athletes to show.

Athletes are matched by position, so the recorded GS's goals are your GS's; anyone with no match is left unnamed and their goals go to the team. Swap puts the teams the other way round, so either can be the winner. Your competition's setup comes from PlayHQ, so a demo needs the internet.
