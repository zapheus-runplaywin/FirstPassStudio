Hear Studio

Set the level with the phone's volume buttons

Output 1's sound and the other mics, without your own voice. Use headphones: on the phone's speaker it comes back in through your mic. It's a moment behind the stream's picture (more on Bluetooth headphones), so use it to hear your co-commentator and the music, not to watch along.

A wired or USB-C mic sounds best. A Bluetooth headset's mic drops to phone-call quality and arrives about a fifth of a second late, so use it only when there's nothing else. Plug in before pressing Start.

While you're on

Keep this page open with the screen on (it stays awake by itself): the phone stops the mic when you switch apps or lock it. Put the phone on Do Not Disturb, since a call takes the mic. Your level and mute are on the Mac in Mics, where you're named after this phone.
