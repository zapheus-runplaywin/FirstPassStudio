YOU

Starts from the clock

Minutes before the game, at quarter, half or three quarter time, or after the final siren, from the PlayHQ scorer or your own.

You stay in charge

Hold a break back for a shot after the siren, or come back from a break early. One press, from the card or the Stream Deck.

Fills the break

Repeats until play resumes, finishing the graphic on screen, then hands back to Live game or to the next sequence.

Brings its own music

Playlists with fades and a start mark, levelled loudness, and music that ducks under a video or an interview on court.

Sells the sponsors

A Sponsor ads step plays the next ads due, taking turns fairly by tier, and its time on screen goes into the proof-of-play report.

One press to take over

A Stream Deck key for every sequence, scene and source, with Next, Pause and Stop. Untick one switch to call every break by hand.
